# How Much Does a Residential Pool Slide Cost?

*The Complete Guide to Pool Slide Prices and Project Costs*

**The slide price is not the project price.** A published price may cover the slide alone, or it may include tax and delivery while excluding installation. Your complete budget also depends on the pool, the deck, the water connection and the work required at your address. No reliable single installation price emerged from the research behind this guide.

If you are comparing prices for a private pool, start by asking what each quoted amount includes. Then obtain a separate assessment for the site work. The examples below are dated public prices for named third-party products. They are not AQUASEE prices, offers or recommendations, and the three currencies and product types cannot be combined into a market average.

## What determines the price of a pool slide?

Start with the exact model or design proposal. Ask for a drawing and a component list so you can see what the price actually covers. Questions worth resolving include:

- **Size and form:** What are the dimensions, slide path, access route and intended exit position of the proposed model?
- **Materials and components:** What are the slide surface, supports, handrails, anchors and fasteners made from? A stainless steel handrail does not establish that the entire slide is stainless steel.
- **Structural design:** Does the offer include only the slide, or also its supporting structure, foundations and site-specific engineering?
- **Surface finish:** Which finish is specified, what preparation or polishing does it require, and what care instructions accompany it?
- **Custom engineering:** Are changes to an existing model included, or must a designer and engineer price them separately?
- **Delivery scope:** Are crating, freight, import procedures, local delivery and installation included for your destination?

The observed prices do not isolate the effect of any single factor. For example, the gap between a standard retail slide and a sculptural stainless steel design cannot be attributed to stainless steel alone. Size, engineering, finish, brand-specific design and included delivery differ too.

AQUASEE's product development direction focuses on design-led stainless steel and aluminium pool slides for residential and boutique leisure environments. A slide should be considered as part of the pool setting, including the architecture, available space and family circulation, rather than chosen from its material label alone. The third-party products in this guide illustrate supplier pricing and scope; they are not AQUASEE models, specifications or prices.

For a stainless steel option, ask for the exact grade, fabrication and maintenance information for the proposed product. [worldstainless guidance on corrosion](https://worldstainless.org/about-stainless/properties/corrosion-properties/) explains that corrosion behavior depends on the environment and type of attack. A “316L” label alone is not a promise of zero maintenance or permanent corrosion resistance. The industry guidance does not certify a specific pool slide.

## The costs beyond the slide

A useful budget separates the seller's scope from work that needs a local quote. Mark any item that does not apply to your project as such only after the selected model and site have been reviewed.

| Budget item | Ask the product supplier or seller | Ask the local project team |
|---|---|---|
| **Product and accessories** | Which model, required parts, documentation and optional components are included? | Which additional items, if any, are needed for the approved site design? |
| **Freight and delivery** | Is the published amount a final delivery quote for your postcode, or an estimate? Does it cover unloading? | Is special access, lifting or movement across the site needed? |
| **Taxes and import costs** | Which taxes are included in the quoted selling price? Who handles export documents? | Which local tax, duty, broker or import charges apply, if any? Confirm with the responsible local party. |
| **Foundation and deck preparation** | What does the model's installation document require? | Can the existing deck or planned foundation meet those requirements, and what work is priced? |
| **Structural anchoring** | Which anchors and supports are supplied, and which are excluded? | Who designs, installs and checks the site connection? |
| **Water supply and plumbing** | What connection or flow does this exact model require? | Can the existing system serve it, or is additional plumbing or equipment needed? |
| **Installation labor** | Is installation included, excluded or available only in certain places? | Who will assemble and install the slide, and what is included in the labor quote? |
| **Pool modification and maintenance** | What operating and care documents, spare parts and written warranty terms are available for the proposed model? | Does the pool edge, deck or landscape need alteration? What inspection and upkeep will the owner arrange? |

One seller's freight estimate cannot be applied to another slide. Likewise, a model's anchor or water-connection instructions cannot be used as a universal installation specification. A [Global Pool Products Bad Axe X6 installation guide](https://global-poolproducts.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/Bad-Axe-X6-Assembly-Installation-Guide-12.pdf) illustrates the kinds of placement, anchoring and plumbing information a model manual can contain. It applies to that named model only. Global Pool Products is an independent third-party manufacturer; the citation does not imply an AQUASEE relationship.

## New pool versus existing pool installation

For a **new pool**, the designer can include a possible slide position, access route, water connection and structural support in the project drawings before construction. Those items still have costs. Ask the pool builder to identify them in its quote and confirm them against the eventual slide model's manual.

For an **existing pool**, the first expense may be investigation: measuring the pool and landing area, checking the deck and its substructure, locating services, and reviewing available water supply. Additional work could follow if the selected model requires a different foundation, anchoring or plumbing arrangement. Splinterworks notes that certain sculptural models may need foundations beside an existing pool deck; its [custom slide FAQ](https://www.splinterworks.com/pool-slides/custom-slide-faqs/) describes that possibility for its own work. It is not a rule for every slide.

Neither route supports a standard “new build” or “retrofit” surcharge. Ask a qualified pool professional to assess the exact model and site against its current manual and applicable local requirements. If the landing conditions or structural support cannot be confirmed, the project budget is not ready for approval. For a broader selection checklist, read [How to Choose a Residential Pool Slide](/buying-guides/choose-a-pool-slide).

## Frequently asked questions

### How much does the slide itself cost?

Published examples vary by product and market. On 18 November 2026, a French retailer showed **€1,201 including VAT** for an AstralPool Straight 1.30 m, but marked it **Out of stock** [P10]. A US retailer showed **$1,499.99**, with **$250 estimated freight separately**, for an S.R.Smith Cyclone [P17]. Splinterworks showed a UK starting price of **£55,950 including UK VAT and delivery but excluding installation** for Cyma [P01]. Check the linked seller pages again before relying on any amount. These examples do not define a global price range.

### What is usually excluded from the quoted price?

There is no universal list. The cited Cyma UK price expressly excludes installation, while the cited Cyclone US page shows freight separately. Ask every seller to mark tax, delivery, foundations, anchors, plumbing, labor and site alterations as **included**, **excluded** or **Quote required**. An unmentioned item is not automatically free.

### Can I add a slide to an existing pool?

Possibly, if a specific model's landing, access, support and water requirements can be met at the measured site. A local pool professional should review the model manual and the existing pool before anyone prices the renovation. The first guide covers the [selection and site-fit questions](/buying-guides/choose-a-pool-slide) in more detail.

### Do I need a separate pump or water supply?

The answer depends on the proposed model and the pool's existing plumbing. Request the model's water-connection instructions and have the local pool contractor assess available supply. Do not add a pump to every budget by default, or assume the current system is sufficient.

### Is a stainless steel pool slide worth the investment?

Compare the proposed design, documented materials, finish, maintenance needs and complete installed scope with alternatives that fit your site. The research does not show a reliable stainless-steel-only price premium or a measurable lifetime return. A named design's starting price is evidence for that product and market, not for all stainless steel slides.

### What information is needed for an accurate quote?

Give the seller your country and delivery postcode, whether the pool is new or existing, approximate pool and deck dimensions, a proposed slide location, design preferences and project timeline. Say whether plans or photographs are available. Describe the deck and known water connection. Ask who will verify the landing area and local requirements. If you lack a drawing, say which measurements still need to be taken.

## Build a budget around your pool

A realistic decision starts with one named slide proposal and two clear scopes: what the product seller will supply, and what a qualified local team must design or build. Record unknown amounts as **Quote required** until you have written offers. That makes price comparisons useful even when one seller includes delivery and another does not.
