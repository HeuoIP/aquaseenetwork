H1: Playground Climbing Nets: A Buyer's Guide to Misting and Shade

Main reader decision: specify a commercial climbing-net package and its interfaces with shade and a separately assessed misting element. Buyer: resort, school, park developer, landscape consultant. Strategy v2.1.2/D-014; climbing/misting/shade are elements of Cool Playgrounds, not independent new series. Outdoor Shade remains an independent direction as well.

Natural semantic associations (editorial phrases, not validated exact queries): playground climbing nets with shade; misting near playground climbing nets; rope climbing playground shade planning; playground rope climbers and misting zones; commercial playground climbing equipment with shade and optional misting; climbing net maintenance alongside misting and shade. Use where a distinct buyer question is answered, no numerical density target or mechanical phrase insertion.

Each H2 opens with a direct answer:
1. What are you buying: a climbing net or a complete rope climber? — component vs system, including shade/mist interface in scope. HUCK and KOMPAN primary pages.
2. How should the climbing challenge match the intended users? — model-specific ages, routes and participation; shade/rest and optional mist must not conceal access issues. CPSC layout/climber guidance.
3. Where should shade fall around playground climbing nets? — time-based shade objectives, approved support interfaces, no canopy load assumptions. CPSC + manufacturer shade planning.
4. Where should misting sit beside a rope climbing playground? — assess drift, wetting and routes separately; no unverified wet-use promise. CDC water guidance + explicitly editorial coordination checklist.
5. What rope, fittings and surface documents should you request? — compare actual design and documentation, exposure to mist/cleaning and shade interfaces; no universal dimensions/mesh size. Primary product pages and manuals.
6. Who maintains the nets, misting and shade? — separate inspection and specialist water-system responsibilities; model-specific schedules. KOMPAN manual + CDC.
7. How should the quote separate climbing, misting and shade? — scope/installer/warranty/commissioning matrix, no invented price range; business inquiry CTA.
8. What else should procurement teams clarify? — nine sourced/editorial buyer questions: retrofit, fixed inspection interval, net repair, attach shade to climber, hose-fed mist approval, rain protection, disabled mist, certification, warranty exposure. No duplicate full answers from main sections.
9. Which evidence supports this guide? — sources, jurisdictions and limits, agent preparation disclosure.
